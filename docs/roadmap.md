# Roadmap

## Foundation

- Repository structure.
- Contributor documentation.
- Architecture overview.
- Extension-point documentation.
- Local development scripts.
- CI for install, lint, test, and build.

## Product Loop

- Create surveys.
- Publish shareable survey links.
- Collect public responses.
- Review response counts and answer summaries.

## Later Scope

- Conditional logic and branching.
- Templates and reusable question blocks.
- Custom branding and themes.
- File upload questions.
- Team collaboration and permissions.
- Webhooks and integrations.
- Exports to CSV, Google Sheets, and analytics tools.
