# Code of Conduct

SurveyPanda contributors are expected to participate respectfully and constructively.

## Expected Behavior

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respect different viewpoints and experience levels.
- Give and accept constructive feedback.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Trolling, insulting comments, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing private information without permission.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns to the project maintainers. Maintainers will review reports and respond with appropriate action.
